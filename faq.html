Getting Started

What client do I need?

You need the World of Warcraft 3.3.5a client (build 12340). Use the client download linked on our website, and avoid mixing it with another installation that has been modified for a different version.

Visit Downloads

How do I get started?

Download the required client, follow the connection instructions, create a LegacyWoW account, and launch the game using the instructions on our site. The How to Play page walks you through the setup.

Read How to Play

How do I create an account?

Open the Create Account page and complete the registration form. Your website registration is separate from any retail World of Warcraft account.

Is LegacyWoW affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment?

No. LegacyWoW is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Blizzard Entertainment.

Connection & Technical Help

How do I set my realmlist?

Follow the How to Play instructions and use the realmlist address below in the appropriate configuration file for your client.

set realmlist logon-legacywow.tail63bcbc.ts.net

Make sure you save the file and that you are editing the correct WoW client installation.

Do I need Tailscale?

The current connection instructions on the website use a Tailscale address. Follow the latest How to Play guide for the required setup and invitation steps. If the connection details change, the website's instructions should be treated as the source of truth.

View connection instructions

The game will not connect. What should I check?

First, confirm that you have the correct 3.3.5a client, that the realmlist is entered exactly as shown, and that any required Tailscale setup is complete. Check that you are launching the intended client folder. If the issue continues, contact support and include the exact error message.

Can I use my existing WoW installation?

You can use a separate, compatible 3.3.5a installation. Keeping a dedicated folder for LegacyWoW is recommended so files or settings from other clients do not interfere.

Where should addons go?

For the 3.3.5a client, addons generally belong in the client folder under Interface\AddOns. Make sure the addon supports the 3.3.5a client; modern Retail addons usually will not work correctly.

Realm & Progression

What is the Icecrown realm progression?

Icecrown is designed to progress through World of Warcraft's expansion-era patches toward Wrath of the Lich King 3.3.5a, alongside custom content and quality-of-life changes. Check the latest News posts for the current patch and release schedule, as dates and content can change.

Is the server completely Blizzlike?

LegacyWoW combines the 3.3.5a client and expansion-era progression with custom content and quality-of-life features. Some systems and experiences may differ from the original official game.

Gameplay & Features

Does LegacyWoW have custom content?

Yes. LegacyWoW includes custom content and quality-of-life changes in addition to its expansion-era progression. Check the homepage and News page for features and content that are currently available.

Are multiboxing and automated bots allowed?

Multiboxing is allowed, but gameplay inputs must be made by the player. Prohibited botting and automated gameplay input are not allowed. Please review the Server Rules for the current policy.

What should I do if I find a bug or exploit?

Do not abuse it or use it to gain an advantage. Stop using the affected mechanic and report the issue to staff with enough detail to help reproduce it safely.

Can I suggest a feature or report a problem?

Yes. Contact the team with a clear description of the suggestion or issue, the character or area involved when relevant, and any error messages or steps needed to reproduce the problem.

Accounts, Rules & Enforcement

Can I share or sell my account?

Account sharing and real-money trading are prohibited under the current Server Rules. Keep your credentials private and do not buy, sell, or trade accounts, characters, gold, or items for real money.

Read the Server Rules

What happens if I break a server rule?

Depending on severity and circumstances, staff may issue a warning, suspension, or permanent ban. Serious violations may result in immediate action. Review the Server Rules before playing.

How can I appeal a suspension or ban?

Contact staff calmly through the available support channel. Include your account or character name, the action you are appealing, and any relevant context. Avoid posting private account details in public channels.

Is Guild War still available?

No. Guild War has been discontinued. Older references to its mechanics may remain in historical documentation, but they should not be taken as confirmation that the mode is active.

Support & Contact

How do I contact the team?

Email Legacy.WoW.team@gmail.com and a GM or team member can assist. Include a concise explanation of the issue and any relevant details.

What information should I include in a support request?

Include the nature of the problem, your character name, when it occurred, the steps that led to it, and the exact error message if there is one. Never send your password.

Where can I find official updates?

The website's News page is the best place to check announcements about progression, events, maintenance, and server changes.
