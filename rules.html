LegacyWoW Community Guidelines

Server Rules

These rules help keep LegacyWoW fair, welcoming, and enjoyable for everyone. Playing on the server means agreeing to follow them. If you are unsure whether something is allowed, ask staff before doing it.

Important: Staff may consider context, intent, severity, and prior violations when deciding how to handle an incident. Attempts to exploit loopholes or evade enforcement may result in additional penalties.

1. Respectful Conduct

  • No harassment, hate speech, discriminatory abuse, targeted intimidation, or sustained personal attacks.
  • Keep chat reasonably civil. Disagreements are allowed; threats, repeated abuse, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not.
  • Do not impersonate staff, other players, or official LegacyWoW representatives.
  • Do not share another player's private or identifying information without their permission.
  • Keep character names, guild names, and public messages appropriate for a shared gaming community.

2. Fair Play & Exploits

  • No cheating, prohibited botting, unauthorized automation, or third-party tools that give an unfair advantage.
  • Multiboxing is allowed, but all gameplay inputs must be made by the player. Automated gameplay input is not allowed.
  • Do not abuse bugs, unintended mechanics, map exploits, or other loopholes to gain an advantage.
  • If you discover a bug or exploit, stop using it and report it to staff. Repeated or deliberate abuse may lead to serious penalties.
  • Do not interfere with server operations, attempt to compromise accounts, or exploit vulnerabilities in the website or server.

3. Accounts & Security

  • Real-money trading (RMT) involving accounts, characters, gold, items, or services is prohibited.
  • Account sharing is not allowed. Keep your account credentials private and secure.
  • You are responsible for activity carried out on your account. Staff may review the circumstances of a suspected violation.
  • Do not attempt to access another player's account or use accounts to evade a suspension or ban.
  • Do not exploit payment, donation, or account systems or misrepresent transactions to obtain an unfair benefit.

4. Chat, Advertising & Spam

  • No spam, chat flooding, or deliberately disruptive repeated messages.
  • Do not advertise cheats, account sales, gold sales, or other prohibited services.
  • Do not use public channels to repeatedly provoke, disrupt, or derail other players' conversations.
  • Keep recruitment, trade, and group-finding messages relevant to the channel and avoid excessive repetition.

5. PvP & Player Interaction

  • Play within the server's intended rules and mechanics. Do not use exploits to manipulate PvP outcomes or rewards.
  • Do not coordinate with opposing players to manipulate battlegrounds, rankings, rewards, or other competitive systems.
  • Do not deliberately obstruct events or other server activities through exploits or behavior intended to prevent others from participating.
  • Normal in-game rivalry and legitimate PvP are part of the game; harassment and exploit abuse are not.

6. Staff & Support

  • Follow reasonable staff instructions related to server rules, investigations, and maintaining a fair environment.
  • Do not harass staff or use tickets to threaten, spam, or make knowingly false reports.
  • If you disagree with an action, appeal calmly through the available support channels rather than disrupting public chat.
  • Do not impersonate staff or claim that a staff member has authorized something when they have not.
  • Report suspected rule violations with useful details when possible. Do not retaliate against or publicly expose players you report.

7. Server Integrity

  • Do not attempt to crash, overload, disrupt, or gain unauthorized access to the game server, website, or related services.
  • Do not exploit registration, launcher, or other server systems to bypass restrictions or obtain unauthorized access.
  • Do not evade disciplinary action by creating or using alternate accounts.
  • Staff may temporarily restrict access or investigate behavior that threatens server stability or community safety.

8. Enforcement

Depending on the circumstances, staff may issue a warning, remove inappropriate content, suspend an account, or permanently ban an account. Severe violations may result in immediate action.

  • Penalties may reflect severity, intent, impact, prior history, and whether a player cooperates with an investigation.
  • Trying to hide, repeat, or benefit from a violation may increase the penalty.
  • Staff decisions should be appealed through support channels. Public arguments or harassment will not help resolve a case.

Guild War Rules (Historical)

Guild War has been discontinued. The following text is retained for historical reference only and does not describe an active game mode. These rules should not be treated as current gameplay instructions.

Guild War was an optional game mode that allowed guilds to compete in the open world for control of key locations. Participation was not required, and the mode did not directly impact players who chose not to opt in.

How to Participate: Historically, a Guild Master purchased a Guildhouse in a major city. Guild members could speak with their assigned Guild Officer inside the Guildhouse to begin attacking, defending, or capturing locations.

Cities: Cities were the largest and most valuable locations in Guild War. They provided income and allowed the controlling guild to set tax rates on player transactions, with revenue deposited into the guild bank. To capture a contested city, a guild acquired Marks of Control and deposited them into the guild bank under the tab corresponding to the target location. At 50% control, the city became theirs. Cities required upkeep and could become vulnerable to siege if control fell below 50%. They could be sieged once per day; a successful defense locked the city against attacks for three days.

Forts: Forts were strategic military positions across the open world. They could initially be claimed using Marks of Control. Opposing guilds could weaken control by raiding the fort and defeating its Officer. After sufficient successful raids, the fort became contested again. Forts required less upkeep than cities and were vulnerable during designated raid windows. They could be attacked five times daily; a successful defense prevented further attacks for 24 hours.

Outposts: Outposts were smaller locations chosen for income or strategic positioning, such as Goldshire. They required minimal upkeep and could be weakened by defeating the stationed Officer or through Marks of Control. Outposts could expand into nearby areas. They had no fixed attack limit, but a successful defense protected them from attacks for 12 hours.

Territories: Territories were adjacent lands influenced by nearby controlled locations. Stable control extended influence into surrounding areas; weakened control could cause areas to revert to neutral. Territories provided minor gold income and acted as a buffer between major holdings.

Additional Rules: Guilds could form alliances. During a siege, players could reinforce another guild by submitting an in-game ticket specifying whom they wished to support and how; reinforcements were subject to associated costs.

Guilds could also be vassalized if they lost all territories or both the Guild Master and officers were defeated in a duel. Vassalized guilds fell under another guild's control or influence. Guild Masters were allowed up to five key officers.

Guilds received NPC bots to assist with offense and defense. Units could join a group, be stationed at controlled locations, or be deployed in attacks. The historical limit was five NPCs per 25 guild members; exceeding it was described as a severe violation that could result in a Guild Master ban and permanent guild disbandment.
