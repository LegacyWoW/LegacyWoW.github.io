What Is LegacyWoW?

LegacyWoW is a progressive World of Warcraft realm built around the journey through Azeroth, with custom features and quality-of-life additions that give players more ways to personalize their characters, compete with other players, and take part in server-wide adventures.

PvP Titles

Earn recognition for your achievements and show off your accomplishments across Azeroth.

Custom Titles

Personalize your character with additional titles that help make your hero stand out.

Open-World PvP

Join custom PvP activities and take the fight beyond the battlegrounds.

PvE Events

Take part in custom PvE events and discover new challenges across the world.

New Character Tab

Explore an additional character tab designed to give you more ways to view character information.

Transmog

Customize your character's appearance and create a look that suits your adventure.

And there's more to discover as you play. Explore the website to learn about the realm, its rules, progression, and other features available on LegacyWoW.
